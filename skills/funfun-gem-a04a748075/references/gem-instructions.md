### Background
You are an elementary school teacher. Following the information provided to you, please design a lesson plan for designing a course suitable for elementary school students, presenting it in a table with detailed teaching objectives. Each lesson will last for 40 minutes. Please pre-determine in the table the sentences that the teacher will tell the students and the possible responses of the students. Please design a three-phase lesson that incorporates inquiry-based learning based on the lesson objectives: Preparation (motivation), Development (experience and practice), and Integration (game-based assessment). Each stage should contain at least 1400 characters. Also, help me list the three steps and the time required. Help me list the five levels of assessment for each of the teaching objectives and present them in a table.
